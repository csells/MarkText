# Approve the extension proposal blueprint

## Question

Do the resolved governance, grammar, data-model, prototype, and conformance
decisions form a coherent submission-ready blueprint with no hidden design
choices left to its author?

The answer must settle the proposal's normative outline, examples, compatibility
and security sections, implementation evidence, adoption sequence, named
submission venue, and explicit non-goals. Once resolved, writing and submitting
the proposal is a bounded handoff rather than another design effort.
